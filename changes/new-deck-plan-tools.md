### Changed

- New… step 4: **the plan no longer means “every part”; it changes how much goes in.** Subjects are chosen on both plans and pre-ticked from step 2 on both (never all ticked by default; the paid “all of them are included” state is gone). Chat AI: free plan sends the standard parts (`FREE_CORE`) and up to two choices, **subjects and tools together** (a tool counts as one: a subject plus a tool falls under the 50 KB pair cap; with two on, the other subject and tool tiles are disabled and 「チャットの AI：無料のプランでは、教科と道具を合わせて 2 つまで選べます」 / “Chat AI: free plan, so up to two subjects and tools in total.” is shown); Chat AI: paid plan sends every standard part and the chosen subjects and tools with no limit. Switching back to the free plan keeps the first two ticks in tile order (subjects first, then tools) and shows the limit message. The strip 「この指示に入るパーツ」 and the answered line follow the chosen subjects and tools on both plans. Instructions pack `fb9e430d` → `48ab84a3`.

### Added

- New… step 4: a **道具 / Tools** row, 「スライドで道具を使いますか」 / “Will you use any tools on the slides?”, with the hint 「選んだ道具だけを、AI が使います」 / “The AI uses only the tools you choose.”, built from the components' manifest (`components.<name>.group: "tool"` and its `tile`; today タイマー / Timer). A tool part goes into the instructions only when its tile is on, on either plan; the timer is pre-ticked when the title, content or other wishes mention グループワーク, a number of minutes (「10 分」) or 時間, never over your own choice. The chosen tools are stored in `studio.newDeck.tools`. `node kit/llm-pack.mjs` takes `--tools`, and `--plan paid` now honours `--series` (`--series all --tools all --plan paid` is the full pack). `npm run gen` checks the free plan with a tool too (a tool alone against 40 KB, every subject + tool against 50 KB), every paid pair of subjects with every tool against 120 KB, and only warns for the paid all-ticked worst case.

### Changed (API)

- `assemble(data, {series, tools, lang, exclude, off, plan})` (`kit/core/llm-pack-assemble.mjs`), `buildPack`, `packWithRequest`, `commonBody(…, plan, tools)`, `seriesPart(…, plan, tools)` and `loadedParts(series, plan, tools)` take an optional `tools` list (tags or names, or `'all'`); without it no tool is included. With `plan: 'paid'`, `series` is now honoured (it used to be every series): pass `series: 'all', tools: 'all'` for the full pack. `kit/core/llm-pack.mjs` exports `TOOLS`; `kit/core/new-deck.mjs` exports `guessTools` and `toolsForChoice`, and `studio.newDeck` has `tools`.
