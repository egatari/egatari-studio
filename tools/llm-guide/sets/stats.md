## Part set: stats (statistics charts)

For lessons and talks with data: distributions, box plots, heatmaps, and a distribution updated one observation at a time. Use the `default` theme. Give the source and the size of every data set on the slide or in the script, and never invent data that looks real (R-components).

- **Axis charts** (<!--part:m-hist-->`m-hist`, <!--/part--><!--part:m-boxplot-->`m-boxplot`, <!--/part--><!--part:m-seq-->`m-seq`, <!--/part-->and the like) reserve 86 px at the left and 80 px in all at the top and bottom for ticks and axis names: give them a height of **300–380 px** and a width of **at least 560 px** (in `<div class="cols">`, give the chart column at least 55%).
- Make the axis ranges contain every plotted value (computed as R-components says).
