### Changed

- Egatari Player 1.11.0 (the bundled player that files carry, `player` in the index and `deck-meta`): minor release. Slides can carry a source line, `<p class="source">…</p>`: placed directly in the slide it sits just above the conclusion line, small and grey, and the body is shortened to make room; inside the body it ends the body (handoff #86)
- Egatari Player 1.11.0 also gives parts `ctx.answer(id, correct, value?)` (format §8.4): the answer is recorded per question (`<slide key>/<index of the part on the slide>/<id>`, the latest replacing earlier ones, answered and answered correctly counted at least once), sent as the tracking event `answer` `{slideKey, id, correct, value?}`, and `metadata.completion.checks` (`"answered"` or `"correct"`, §9.2) must hold as well for completion. In an LMS the player sends `cmi.core.score.min` 0, `max` 100 and `raw` (the share of questions with a right answer answered correctly) and keeps the answered and correct questions in `cmi.suspend_data` as `qa` and `qc` (SCORM 1.2 binding §2.1); passed and failed are never sent (handoff #84)
