# Narration: City A budget for 2027

## City A budgetfor 2027 {#s-erx4ug}
[0] Here is City A's budget for 2027: where your taxes go. The city and all the figures are fictional.

## What the city spends in a year {#s-cor2aj}
[0] First, how much the city spends in a year.
[1] The total budget is 62 billion yen, 2.1 percent more than last year.
[2] That is about 413 thousand yen for each resident.
[3] City A has 150 thousand residents.
[concl] The city spends about 413 thousand yen for each resident.

## What the money is for {#s-snmnh4}
[0] So what is the money for?
[1] Welfare is the largest share, more than a third, followed by education, administration, and roads and the town.
[concl] Welfare is the largest share.

## If the budget were ¥10,000 {#s-w2caut}
[0] Big numbers are hard to picture, so let us scale the whole budget down to ten thousand yen.
[1] Welfare gets 3,700 yen.
[2] Education gets 1,200.
[3] Administration gets 1,200.
[4] Roads and the town get 1,100.
[5] Debt repayment gets 1,000.
[6] Health and waste get 900.
[7] And other spending gets 900.
[concl] Of every ten thousand yen, 3,700 goes to welfare.

## What changed from last year {#s-nnpx5j}
[0] What went up, and what went down, compared with last year?
[1] Welfare grew by 0.93 billion yen and education by 0.42 billion. Roads and the town fell by 0.33 billion, and debt repayment by 0.18 billion.
[concl] More went to welfare and schools, and less to roads and debt.

## Where the money comes from {#s-g63pih}
[0] And where does the money come from?
[1] City taxes paid by residents bring in about a third. The rest comes from national and prefectural grants, the national tax allocation, borrowing and other sources.
[concl] City taxes pay about a third, and grants and loans pay the rest.

## How to have your say {#s-77gyo7}
[0] There are ways to have your say on the budget.
[1] You can ask questions at a budget briefing, send your views through the city's feedback form, or watch the city council, which decides the budget.
[concl] The council decides the budget, after hearing from residents.
