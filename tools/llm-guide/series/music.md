## Series pack: music (pitch, scales, chords)

For music lessons: pitch, scales, intervals and chords on a piano keyboard. Use the `default` theme. Name notes as the class does (ドレミ, C D E, or ハニホ) and keep the same names on the slide and in the narration. The keyboard also sounds the notes; never let the sound carry anything the lit keys do not show.
