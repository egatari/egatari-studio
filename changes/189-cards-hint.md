### Added

- Quality check: a text slide whose body is only 3 or 4 short bullets of similar length gets an information finding, “Short, equal points may read better as cards (m-cards)” (`cardsHint`, R-purpose, backlog #189). The body must hold exactly one unordered list (and at most one short lead paragraph) and nothing else (no ordered list, nested list, part, figure, image, table or display equation); each item is at most 30 characters (Japanese decks count full-width 1 and half-width 0.5, as elsewhere in the check) and the longest is at most twice the shortest. A list is not suggested when an item has inline math, or when the items read as a sequence (an item starts with まず・次に・そこで・最後に or first/next/then/finally, or contains →). Cover, section divider, `static` and vertical slides are skipped. It is information only: not a warning, and never part of fix requests, so the author decides. The editor's quality check shows it too. The AI instructions and generated packs are unchanged
