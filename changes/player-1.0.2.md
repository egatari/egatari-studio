### Changed

- Egatari Player 1.0.2 (the bundled player that files carry, `player` in the index and `deck-meta`): the pen colors no longer follow the theme; they are fixed red #FF4B00, blue #005AFF and black (the color-universal-design set), so pen lines stay visible on every theme's background. The presenter view shows the same colors. Files from earlier versions keep their own player.
