Exponential decay

The decay law

Let \(N(t)\) be the number of atoms left at time \(t\).

\[ N(t)=\htmlId{n0}{N_0}\,e^{-\htmlId{lam}{\lambda} t} \]
  • \(N_0\): the number at the start
  • \(\lambda>0\): the decay constant (fraction lost per second)
The half-life \(\ln 2/\lambda\) does not depend on \(N_0\)

A larger λ decays faster

\[ \frac{N(t)}{N_0}=e^{-\lambda t} \]
  • Grey: the previous \(\lambda\)
Raising λ shortens the half-life
