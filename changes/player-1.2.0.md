### Changed

- Egatari Player 1.2.0 (the bundled player that files carry, `player` in the index and `deck-meta`): minor release. Part sounds are scheduled ahead on the Web Audio clock at their exact times (the whole step when it starts, and in autoplay the next step too) and cancelled on back, jump, seek, mute, pause, a manual step and `ctx.extend`; autoplay follows one absolute clock instead of re-anchoring every step. A hand-made file carrying a 2.x part without the registry no longer throws `egatariParts is not defined`: the element is reported in `DECK.componentErrors` and shown as its text equivalent. Printing no longer adds a blank last page. A component's data error in the quality check gives the type of every field. Files from earlier versions keep their own player.
