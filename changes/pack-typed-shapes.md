### Changed
- The data shapes in the AI instructions (catalogue column "data shape") now give the type of every field, optional ones too, in a short form: `dash?:bool`, `diff?:str`, enums as `"a"|"b"`, lists as `T[]`, tuples as `[num,num]`. Models no longer guess (`dash?` written as `"dashed"`, `rows[].diff` as `true`). To keep the free plan within 40 KB, the six fields every part shares (label, tex, note, tone: str; step: int; at: num) are typed once at the start of §3 and written by name only. A component's data error in check and in the fix request shows every type in the same form (`kit/shape.js` `brief`).
