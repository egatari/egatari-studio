### Added

- Live room (draft, format extension `live`): Export ▾ → **Distribute with a live room…** adds a room of [egatari/live](https://github.com/egatari/live) (Q&A for the whole lesson, polls per step) to the play-only copy; `node kit/build.mjs <deck> --play-only --live room.json` does the same. The copy gets a CSP `<meta>` whose `connect-src` is exactly the room service, an `extensions` block and the live player (`kit/live.js`); copies without a room are byte-identical to before. The lesson keeps the settings without the key (`extensions.live` in `egatari.json` and the index); the editor connects nowhere
- Live player: a pane outside the stage (the stage is fitted into the rest), a notice and one tap on Join before the first connection, a persistent indicator with Disconnect, polls when their step is reached, reconnects with backoff only for retryable closes inside the window, and no network at all outside the window, before Join, in previews and frames, in video mode, in print and in the presenter view. `tools/test-live.mjs` tests it against a local server speaking the vendored protocol (`vendor/egatari-live-protocol/`)
