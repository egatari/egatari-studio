### Changed

- Egatari Player 1.13.0 (the bundled player that files carry, `player` in the index and `deck-meta`): minor release. When the bookmark buttons do not fit the toolbar, they collapse into one **Bookmarks ▾** button that opens a list of the names (handoff #90). On a touch screen, a sideways swipe on the stage or a tap near its left or right edge goes to the previous or next step (handoff #97). The presenter view has **Previous** and **Next** buttons under the current slide, so it can be moved on with a click, an Apple Pencil (Sidecar on an iPad) or a finger; during a blackout they only end it, as the keys do (handoff #104). In Japanese, the blackout is now called 暗転: the presenter view says 暗転中 (was 黒い画面中), and so do the corner notice, the key list and the docs; the English wording is unchanged (handoff #105). The items of the presenter view's top bar no longer wrap inside: when they do not fit on one line, the bar drops the words “Slide” and “Step” (showing 2 / 6 · 0 / 4) and shows Autoplay and Close presenter view as icons with their names as tooltips, and if that is still too wide it wraps onto a second row (handoff #105)
