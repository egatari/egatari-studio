### Added

- Components v1.13.0 is the default version: **`m-heatmap`** (ヒートマップ: a grid of cells coloured by value, such as a confusion or correlation matrix), in the series `academic`, and **`m-map-world`** (世界地図: countries and regions, categories and values, points and arrows on a Pacific-centred world map; map data from Natural Earth, public domain), in the series `social`. Both are paid-only for now (`FREE_OUT`). `m-tree` gains branch labels and paths (paid only), `m-flow` gains decision diamonds and loops, and `m-bars` gains negative values and `stack="percent"`. Release notes: [egatari/components v1.13.0](https://github.com/egatari/components/releases/tag/v1.13.0)

### Changed

- Components v1.13.0, **visible in lessons**: `m-bars` draws bars with negative values below the zero line (before, a negative value was drawn as 0).
