### Added
- Components v1.2.0 is the default version (1.0.0–1.1.0 are kept for lessons that recorded them). Eight new parts: `m-compare`, `m-venn`, `m-tree`, `m-fishbone`, `m-scatter`, `m-pie`, `m-timeline` and `m-cycle`. For now they are in the instructions only under the paid plan (New…, “Paid plan”); under the free plan, no text names them and the purpose table falls back to “not in this text: the nearest part, or HTML or inline SVG” for their purposes. `m-kpi` has presentation variants (`count`, `rise`, `flip`, `underline`) chosen with `variant` (format spec §8.4). `m-bars` has `stack` (stacked bars). Release notes: [egatari/components v1.2.0](https://github.com/egatari/components/releases/tag/v1.2.0)
- Free plan: `FREE_CORE` (`kit/core/llm-pack-assemble.mjs`) lists the common parts whose notes go into the free common body (`m-bars`, `m-flow`, `m-menu`, the v1.1.0 set). `m-curves`, `m-kpi` and `m-quad`, now common in the components, come in only with their v1.1.0 subjects under the free plan (`FREE_HOME`: Math and statistics, Business, Business). The paid plan has every part. `npm run gen` prints the size of each plan (free per subject and with all subjects, paid), and `node kit/llm-pack.mjs --plan paid` prints the paid pack.
- A part's notes that name another part (“to compare values by aspect, use `m-compare`”) leave that clause out when the other part is not in the copied text (a purpose turned off, a subject not chosen, or a paid-only part); the “do not use” line of New… names only parts that are in the copied text.

### Changed
- `m-flow`: edge labels and the text in `muted` boxes now use `--fg` (they used `--muted`, which was faint when projected). Existing lessons that use them look different when they are rebuilt with v1.2.0.
- The instructions keep only the names of a part's presentation variants (the AI does not need to write them), and shorten how stacked bars are drawn. Instructions pack `ff02abc6` → `26de0e58`.

### Changed (API)
- `assemble(data, {series, lang, exclude, off, plan})`, `buildPack({…, plan})`, `packWithRequest({…, plan})`, `commonBody(lang, exclude, series, off, plan)` and `seriesPart(name, lang, exclude, plan)`: `plan` is `'free'` (default; `FREE_CORE`, `FREE_HOME`, no paid-only parts) or `'paid'` (every series and every part; `series` is ignored). New: `packParts`, `planData`, `paidOnly`, `freeSeriesOf`, `goneParts` (`kit/core/llm-pack-assemble.mjs`), `PAID_ONLY`, `loadedParts(series, plan)` and `membersOfSeries(name, plan)` (`kit/core/llm-pack.mjs`), `hidden` in `purposesTable`, and `node kit/llm-pack.mjs --plan paid`.
