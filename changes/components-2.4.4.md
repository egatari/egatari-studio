### Changed

- Components 2.0 lessons use egatari/components **v2.4.4** (candidate) by default. `m-map-japan` and `m-map-world` 2.0.3: in an English lesson (`ctx.lang` not `ja`) name and value chips are as tall as the English font's text box (Source Sans 3: 1.43 em), fixing a 3 px `svgTextBox` overflow; `m-map-japan` also writes its built-in island names and inset captions in English (Takeshima, Senkaku Islands, Okinotorishima, Minamitorishima, Chichijima, Iwo To, Nansei Islands, Ogasawara Islands) instead of overflowing their label boxes in the English font. Both parts now declare `static glyphs` so a built lesson keeps those built-in characters (including the English names) when it subsets its fonts. Japanese lessons draw as before. No other part changes version. The AI instructions are unchanged apart from their version line
