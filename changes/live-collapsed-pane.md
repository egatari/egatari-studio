### Changed

- Live player: the pane starts collapsed as a small **Live** marker at the top right (a button with `aria-expanded`), and the slides use the whole window. Tapping the marker opens the notice and **Join** before joining, and the question box once connected, on any slide. On a step with a poll the pane opens by itself (announced in a live region, without taking the focus) and collapses again when that step is left, unless the learner pinned it (**Keep open**) or opened it from the marker. While connected the collapsed marker shows the state (a green dot, and text for screen readers); **Disconnect** is in the opened pane. When open, the pane is docked beside or below the slides as before, so it never covers them. The fold strip is gone; **×** or Esc collapses the pane. Reduced motion is respected, and the zero-network rules are unchanged
