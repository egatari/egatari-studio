### Added

- Quality check: `balance` (R-balance, phase 1, info only). On each slide's final step the body (`.body`) is rasterised and the pixels that differ from the slide background are measured: the vertical offset of their centre of mass from the optical centre (45% from the top), the horizontal offset from the middle, and the one-sided empty band (the largest difference between opposite margins) with its side. A slide is reported when its content sits high (centre of mass more than 10% of the body height above the optical centre) and the band below is more than 25% larger than the band above (`layout-tate`: content to the right and the left empty). Even margins all round score about 0. The message gives the numbers only. Cover, section divider and `static` slides are skipped; the conclusion line is outside the body and not measured. The finding never counts as an error or warning and is left out of fix requests (`kit/core/fill-measure.mjs`, `kit/fill-scan.mjs`)
- `node tools/fill-report.mjs <deck.html | folder …> [--json]` prints the vertical and horizontal offsets, the one-sided band and its side and whether the slide is flagged, per slide, for tuning the thresholds on a set of lessons
