### Fixed

- Player on a phone held upright (about 390 px wide): the toolbar no longer runs off the screen; it wraps onto two rows. Mobile browsers no longer widen the page to the unscaled slide (to about 660 px), so the slide scales to the screen width instead of being cut off at both sides. Files from earlier versions keep their own player.
- On a touch device (`(hover: none)`), the one-time toolbar hint now reads "Tap the screen to show the toolbar"; with a mouse it is unchanged.
