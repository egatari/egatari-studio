### Added

- Themes: a new colour role `--lit`, light and what it lights (the sun, a lamp, the lit side of the Moon; format spec §8.4). It is a fill colour drawn with an outline, never a text colour, and a part that uses it gives a fallback from the other roles (`var(--lit, …)`). Every bundled theme sets a warm yellow: `default` `#F5B800` (1.3.0), `sumi` and `business` `#F2C300`, `kokugo` `#F8B500`, `tokiwa` `#F5C000`, `murasaki` `#F3BE00`, `kogecha` `#EDB91A`, `asagi` `#FFCE3A` (each 1.1.0). No existing colour changes; earlier versions stay in `vendor/themes/`. `node tools/test-themes.mjs` checks that `--lit` stays apart from `--emph` under colour-vision simulation with the limits used for `--emph` against `--term`, and prints its contrast with `--bg`
- Quality check: colours outside the theme's vocabulary in the source are reported (`colorOutside`, R-colors, a warning): colours written directly (hex, `rgb()` `oklch()` and the other colour functions, colour names, a literal fallback in `var()`) and colours derived by changing the hue with relative colour syntax (`oklch(from var(--accent2) l c calc(h + 30))`, and likewise `rgb` `hsl` `hwb` `lab` `lch` `oklab` `color()`). `var(--lit)` and the other roles, `color-mix()` of roles and `--bg`, and relative colours that keep the hue and change only lightness, chroma or alpha are not reported
- AI instructions: the colour list gains one line, “`--lit`: light and what it lights, a fill with an outline”. In the light English instructions with the maps set, Matrix (`m-quad`) is now also left out to stay within the paste length
