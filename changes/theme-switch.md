### Added

- Editor app: **Slide ▾ → Change theme…** changes the theme of an open deck. It lists the themes of New… ③ (display names, color swatches, the current one marked, own themes under My themes, and Load theme…, which opens the same Load theme dialog and adds what it loads to the list), rewrites only `data-theme` on the source's `<html>` (other attributes such as `data-egatari-seed` and the slides stay), pins the new theme and the themes it builds on (`extends`) at their current versions (a theme of your own, from the theme folder or a ZIP file, at its `theme.json` version or `"local"`; it is found in the same place as the build looks) in `egatari.json` `themes`, even over an earlier pin, and drops pins nothing uses (other members keep their bytes).
  The preview is rebuilt; it is an unsaved change and **Undo** in the slide list restores the source and `egatari.json` byte for byte. Disabled, with the reason, in files where only narration, audio and timing can be edited.
