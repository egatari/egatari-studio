### Added

- 17 more samples on the first screen, copied unmodified from egatari/examples (the gallery lessons of egatari.org except the commercial): Egatari の紹介 (ja and en), パーツ図鑑, the moon's phases, chords, sentence structure, Huffman codes, Bayes' theorem, Japan's population, the shared-birthday class vote, the generative AI guideline, a quarterly report, security training, a city budget (ja and en), hand hygiene and the hazard map. 16 of them work with 「このサンプルを参考に作る」 and `?from-sample=<name>`; パーツ図鑑 only plays (its name opens New… with the not-found line). `tools/examples-sync.mjs` copies them from a fixed commit with every file's SHA-256 in `SOURCE.json`, and `--check` (in `npm run gen:check`) refuses an edited copy

### Changed

- The samples on the first screen are cards instead of one long list. The top row 「まず見る」 has Egatari の紹介 (the screen's language first; the other one carries a language label) and パーツ図鑑 (play it, or open the parts list). Below it are the cards for each kind of sample (サンプルの種類), 教育 (授業・講義・校内研修), ビジネス (報告・提案・社内研修) and 生活 (お知らせ・手引き・啓発), with the same kinds and wording as egatari.org's gallery (`examples/index.json`; `tools/examples-sync.mjs --check --lessons` compares them). A kind with 7 or more samples is split under group headings (教育: 授業, 大学の講義・学会発表, 校内研修), and class samples go in subject order (国語・社会・数学・理科・英語・音楽・情報), as on the site. Each sample is a tile with a thumbnail (サムネイル: the same picture as on egatari.org, a step that shows what the lesson does; Studio-only samples have a picked one), its title, its setting (「授業・理科」 when it has a subject), labels (本, 声, ライブ for a lesson built for a live session (a part with a poll id), 縦書き, and the lesson's language when it differs from the screen's) and up to three tags (タグ: the parts it uses). A line says 「タグを押すと、そのパーツを使ったサンプルだけを並べます。」; pressing a tag lists those samples under 「流れ図を使ったサンプル（9 件）」 with 「絞り込みを外す」, and `?samples-part=<tag>` opens the same filter (for links from the parts list). The button on each tile is still 「再生」
- Glossary: new rows sample-tag (タグ), sample-kind (サンプルの種類), thumbnail (サムネイル) and sample-label (ラベル); play-sample also covers egatari.org's gallery
