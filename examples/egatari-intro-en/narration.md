# Narration: Introducing Egatari

## Visuals narrate. You pass it on. {#s-gyslzb}
[0] Visuals narrate. You pass it on. Egatari, which means picture-telling, is one file for everyone with something to tell.

## Figures build in the order you speak {#s-fu7qxa}
[0] Take a quarterly report from a fictional company.
[1] First, three key numbers: sales, members and churn.
[2] Then the bars show how sales grew, quarter by quarter.
[concl] Each click brings in the next part, in the order you speak.

## Slides and a book, in one file {#s-xscp5m}
[0] An Egatari file has two faces.
[1] The slides are for showing while you talk.
[2] The book tells the same story in full text, for reading.
[3] Press r, and the book of this introduction opens.
[concl] What you show and what people read live in the same file.

## The AI need not get it right the first time {#s-csvids}
[0] A chat AI can write the slides for you. But the instructions it follows are long and detailed, so it may not get everything right at once.
[1] Say you ask for six slides on your fire drill.
[2] The AI writes them.
[3] Egatari's quality check examines them at once. This is a real finding: the body of slide two runs one line too long, so cut one line.
[4] All you do is copy the finding and paste it to the AI.
[5] The AI cut one line, and the slides passed the quality check.
[concl] The quality check says what to fix and how, so you can finish even if you are new to working with an AI.

## Anywhere there is a browser {#s-ganagi}
[0] What you get is a single HTML file.
[1] It opens on a computer, a tablet or a phone.
[2] It opens with no app to install, even offline.
[3] Send it by email, or put it on a course site.
[4] Export it as a video, in time with the voice.
[5] Run it live, and participants can ask questions and vote.
[6] And whoever receives it can open it and edit it.
[concl] Hand over one HTML file, and it plays.

[0] This introduction was made with Egatari, too. An AI wrote it from a short brief, and it passed the quality check.
[1] The samples are at egatari dot org, Studio runs in your browser, and the format is open for anyone to use.
[concl] Put what you have to tell into one file.
