### Added

- Components v1.11.0 is the default version: **`m-keyboard`** (鍵盤: scales, chords and intervals lit on a piano keyboard, with the notes as component sounds), in the new series `music`, and **`m-kakijun`** (書き順 of kana, stroke by stroke), in the series `kokugo`. `m-keyboard` comes with the subject Music on both plans; `m-kakijun` is paid-only for now (`FREE_OUT`). `m-kakijun`'s licence is `MIT AND OFL-1.1` (its stroke data is derived from strokesvg / Klee One): it is recorded in the vendored manifest and `NOTICE`, and the hosted editor ships the part's `OFL.txt`, `LICENSE-strokesvg.txt` and `strokes-CHANGES.md` under `vendor/components/kakijun/<version>/`. `m-timeline` gains project-plan (Gantt) options: `after` arrows, `progress`, `today` and `unit`. Release notes: [egatari/components v1.11.0](https://github.com/egatari/components/releases/tag/v1.11.0)

### Changed

- Components v1.11.0, **visible in lessons in the lecture view only** (“Show later steps faintly”): every part shows its later steps faintly at the theme's `--preview-opacity`, except `m-predict`, `m-hissan` and `m-timer`, which declare `static preview = "none"` and show nothing ahead. With no lesson seed, a part without `variant` shows its first variant (no change for lessons without a seed). Drawing fixes: the same step now leaves the same DOM whatever was drawn before (`m-cycle`, `m-codetree`, `m-seq`, `m-sweep`, `m-hist`, `m-map-japan`, `m-menu`), and `m-tree`, `m-venn` and `m-numberline` no longer rasterize differently after being redrawn. The expired known flake for macOS `m-cycle` (slide 1, step 5) is removed from `tools/step-reset-known.mjs`.
