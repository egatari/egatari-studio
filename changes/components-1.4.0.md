### Changed

- Components v1.4.0: **`m-kanbun` now gives one step per line (句) of the reading order** (before, one step for the whole reading order): the default `phases="kunten order kaki"` gives 訓点 1 + one step per line read + 書き下し文 1, and a return to an earlier line by a 返り点 or a 再読文字 starts a step of its own. **An existing lesson that updates to this version gets stale steps**: its narration `[n]` must be re-aligned (one `[n]` per 句), and audio recorded for those steps no longer matches. `order-by="all"` keeps the old single step, with the same drawing and timing as v1.3.0. Lessons that recorded an earlier version stay on it until their author updates. The kokugo series instructions and the worked example now write one `[n]` per 句, and `examples/kokugo-kanbun` uses the new default (its narration has one `[n]` per 句 instead of `{cue}`).
  Also fixed in v1.4.0: the current-character highlight of `m-kanbun` fades out within its step, and the `m-kobun` translation lines finish within the step. Release notes: [egatari/components v1.4.0](https://github.com/egatari/components/releases/tag/v1.4.0)
