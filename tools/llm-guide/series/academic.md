## Series pack: academic

For lessons in mathematics, science and statistics: a function that changes with a parameter, several curves on the same axes, data that arrives one observation at a time, many items summarised into a few numbers. Use the `default` theme.

- **Axis charts** (`m-sweep`, `m-curves`, `m-seq`) reserve 86 px at the left and 80 px in all at the top and bottom for ticks and axis names: give them a height of **300–380 px** and a width of **at least 560 px** (in `<div class="cols">`, give the chart column at least 55%).
- Make the axis ranges contain every plotted value (computed as R-components says).
