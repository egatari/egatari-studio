### Fixed
- Tight timing for musical steps. Video: clip boundaries are the cumulative start times rounded to whole frames (each step within one frame of exact time, no drift over many steps; before, per-step rounding added up, e.g. 4 frames over 50 measures at ♩=88 in 2/4), and part sounds sit at the exact cumulative time. Live: part sounds are scheduled ahead on the Web Audio clock at their exact times, the whole step when it starts (rolling, up to 30 s ahead) and in autoplay the next step too, so a stalled main thread does not make a note late (before, up to one animation frame late), and what has not sounded yet is cancelled on back, jump, seek, mute, pause of autoplay, a manual step and `ctx.extend`. Autoplay: step ends follow one absolute clock (start + planned lengths + extensions) instead of re-anchoring every step, and the next step's first sounds are scheduled from the end of the current one. Player files change.
