### Added

- Themes can declare their frame geometry with `metrics` in `theme.json` (`"v": 1`; body, conclusion line and vertical columns in stage pixels of 1280×720), inherited field by field through `extends`. The quality checker (command and editor) derives the body limits (`bodyBottom`, `bodyRight`, `tateBodyHead`, `bodyConclCol`, `bodyLeft`) from it, so a theme that moves these boxes is checked against the right limits; margins, minimum text sizes and title and conclusion lengths do not change. Without `metrics` the defaults reproduce the previous limits exactly. Players never use it to draw, and `theme.json` counts toward the theme's `sha256` ([contract §12](docs/contract.md#12-themes-and-the-layout-css), [reference](docs/reference.md#themes))
- `themeMetricsDrift` (warning, once per deck): the rendered boxes differ from the theme's declaration by more than 1px, or a theme without `metrics` (or the deck's own `<style>`) moves them. Unusable `metrics` values are a build warning (`theme-metrics: …`, reported as `themeMetricsInvalid`) and fall back to the parent's value
- The boxes are measured only with the bundled fonts loaded, never with a fallback font; otherwise the comparison is skipped with one `metricsFontLoad` warning
- `node tools/theme-metrics.mjs measure <theme>` prints the `metrics` block measured from the rendered theme, in whole pixels rounded outward to the more lenient limit (`check` compares it with the declaration). `tools/test-theme-metrics.mjs` fails when a bundled theme renders differently from its declared (or default) geometry
