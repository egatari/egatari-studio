### Changed

- Egatari Player 1.13.0 (the bundled player that files carry, `player` in the index and `deck-meta`): minor release. When the bookmark buttons do not fit the toolbar, they collapse into one **Bookmarks ▾** button that opens a list of the names (handoff #90). On a touch screen, a sideways swipe on the stage or a tap near its left or right edge goes to the previous or next step (handoff #97). The presenter view has **Previous** and **Next** buttons under the current slide, so it can be moved on with a click, an Apple Pencil (Sidecar on an iPad) or a finger; during a blackout they only end it, as the keys do (handoff #104)
