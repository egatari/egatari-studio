### Changed

- Components v1.6.0 is the default version (the tarball is the same as v1.5.0; only the release manifest adds tile fields to its purposes). **The purpose tiles in New… are built from the manifest**: `tile: "own"` makes a tile with its `label`, `examples` and `icon`, `{"into": id}` folds a purpose into another tile (ranking into compare: turning the tile off turns both off), `"none"` makes none. The 14 tiles read as before, plus a 15th, **場所・地図 / Place / map** (日本地図・塗り分け・地点と矢印), backed by `m-map-japan` only when the Maps subject is in the instructions; otherwise the AI draws it in HTML, as for any tile without a part. A manifest without tile fields (before v1.6.0) is mapped onto the same 15 tiles by Studio's table, with a warning from `npm run gen`. The hint under the tiles reads 「今は全部使えます。押した表し方は、AI が使いません」 / “All are in use. Press one and the AI won't use that representation.” Instructions pack `0f5e51c9` → `8e0efb1a`; the largest free pair is ja 49,079 B (academic + kokugo; cap 51,200 B), paid ja 92,900 B. Release notes: [egatari/components v1.6.0](https://github.com/egatari/components/releases/tag/v1.6.0)
