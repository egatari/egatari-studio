### Added

- Viewer: notes in the learner's own words, one per slide (typed in the memo area under the slide, with handwriting over it; `m` or 「メモ」) and one per section of the book (opened from a button at the section's end, written in the page, never in the file's frame). Kept on the device only, never on a shared device.
- Viewer: quotes — 「メモ」 in the window for selected words highlights them and quotes them into the note, with ↗ back to the place. Highlights and 暗記 now work on the slides' text too, so Markup shows for files without a book.
- Viewer: export of notes as a PDF handout (each slide with its memo area; printed from a frame made only for printing), as Markdown, and as a backup (`.ega-notes.json`, with highlights and handwriting) that another device can read in for the same file.
